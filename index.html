Our Rich History

Fortunato F. Halili National Agricultural School (FFHNAS) traces its roots to the former Philippine National Agricultural School located in Barangay Guyong, Santa Maria, Bulacan.

The institution was officially renamed through Republic Act No. 7331, approved on March 31, 1992, in honor of Fortunato F. Halili, a respected leader and public servant in Bulacan. This marked an important milestone in strengthening the school's identity as a national agricultural institution.

Since its establishment, FFHNAS has remained committed to providing quality agricultural education and practical training to students. The school was founded to support the agricultural needs of the community and continues to promote sustainable farming, technical skills development, and academic excellence.

Over the decades, the school expanded its programs to include Junior High School, Senior High School, and technical-vocational courses, while preserving its strong agricultural foundation.

Key Milestones:

• Pre-1992: Known as Philippine National Agricultural School
• March 31, 1992: Renamed through Republic Act No. 7331
• 2016: Implementation of the K–12 Senior High School Program
• Present: Continues to serve as a leading agricultural education institution in Santa Maria, Bulacan.

Mission & Vision

Our Vision:

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

Our Mission:

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

• Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment
• Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner
• Administrators and staff ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning
• Family, community, and stakeholders are actively engaged in developing life-long learners

Core Values:

Maka-Diyos • Maka-Tao • Makakalikasan • Makabansa

School Policies & Rules

1. Attendance and Punctuality

Students are expected to attend all classes regularly and on time. Absences exceeding 20% of the total number of school days in a semester may result in being dropped from the subject.

2. Dress Code and Uniform

Students must wear the prescribed school uniform from Monday to Friday. The uniform must be clean, properly worn, and include the school ID at all times while on campus.

3. Academic Integrity

Cheating, plagiarism, and any form of academic dishonesty are strictly prohibited. Students who violate these policies will face appropriate disciplinary action.

4. Use of Mobile Devices

Mobile phones and other electronic devices should be kept on silent mode during class hours. Devices must not be used for non-academic purposes during class time.

5. Campus Conduct

Students must maintain respectful behavior toward teachers, staff, and fellow students at all times. Vandalism, bullying, fighting, and any form of harassment are prohibited.

6. School Property

Students are responsible for the proper use and care of school facilities, equipment, and materials. Any damage caused must be reported immediately and may result in repair or replacement costs.

7. Prohibited Items

Bringing dangerous weapons, illegal substances, alcohol, cigarettes, and other prohibited items on campus is strictly forbidden and will result in immediate disciplinary action.

8. Visitor Policy

All visitors must register at the security office and obtain a visitor's pass before entering the campus. Students are not allowed to bring unauthorized visitors onto school premises.
